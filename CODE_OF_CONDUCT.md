# Code of Conduct

This is a personal catalogue with one maintainer and a deliberately narrow
contribution surface — [issues only, no pull requests](CONTRIBUTING.md). So this
is short, and it is what will actually be enforced rather than a longer document
that would not be.

## The standard

Be civil and be specific. Disagreement about a measurement, a claim, or a
citation is the point of this repository and is always welcome — **especially
when it says an entry is wrong.** Ten of the twelve entries were revised because
evidence contradicted them, and a reader who arrives with a contradicting result
is doing the most valuable thing available here.

Not welcome: personal attacks, harassment, demeaning comments, or sustained
hostility toward anyone taking part. Also not welcome, and more likely in a
repository like this one: dismissing someone's result without engaging with what
they ran.

## Scope

Issues, security advisories, and any other interaction in this repository.

## Enforcement

Report by email to **langej117@gmail.com**, or through
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/langej117/not-evidence/security/advisories/new)
if you would rather it not be public at any point.

I read everything sent here, but this is maintained in personal time and I
cannot promise a response window. Responses are at my discretion and range from
a request to rephrase, through comment deletion, to blocking. I will say which
and why rather than acting silently.

I am also bound by this. If I am the problem, saying so publicly in an issue is
legitimate and will not be deleted for being uncomfortable.
